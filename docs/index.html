A few things to know

Does the app archive websites on my phone?

Your ArchiveBox server performs the archiving and stores the files. The Android app sends links, searches your collection, and opens server pages. Keep your server running and reachable.

Can I connect over Tailscale?

Yes, when your device is connected to your tailnet and the server is reachable. In Connection Settings, check a server’s MagicDNS name or tailnet IP on port 5759. Android cannot enumerate every tailnet peer automatically; provide the names you want to check.

Where is the Google Play download?

The first distribution route is the GitHub beta APK. Google Play availability requires a Play Console listing, production signing, and review. The download button links to GitHub until a listing is published.

How does this compare with the Apple app?

The Android app shares the core save, tag, browse, search, and server connection flows. Apple-specific features have different Android equivalents or remain future work. See the feature comparison for the current scope.

What happens to my data?

Shared URLs and searches go to your configured server. The app stores the connection on your device. Your server controls archive retention and access, and the pages you open may contact their own services. Read the privacy notes.
